📄 Word (.docx) to PDF

Description: Instantly convert your Word documents (.docx) to PDF format. This tool preserves formatting and layout perfectly, making your documents portable and shareable.

How to Use:

  • Click "Select Word File" and choose your .docx file
  • Click "Convert & Download PDF"
  • Your PDF will be generated and downloaded automatically
  • Maximum file size: 5 MB

What the tool does

This tool converts a Microsoft Word document (.docx) into a PDF while preserving the formatting and layout as closely as possible. It is helpful for sending documents that should look the same on every device.

How to use it

  1. Select a .docx file from your device.
  2. Click "Convert & Download PDF".
  3. The browser converts the Word file and downloads the PDF automatically.

Supported file formats

Input: Word document (.docx). Output: PDF (.pdf).

Maximum file size

5 MB per document.

Privacy and security

This conversion happens in the browser, so your document is not uploaded to a server or permanently stored by the site. This makes it a safer option for handling personal or business documents.

When/why someone would use it

Use this tool when you need to send a document as a fixed-layout file, protect formatting across devices, or prepare files for print, email, or sharing with clients.

Short FAQ

Q: Does it keep the same formatting?

A: It keeps the structure and layout as close as possible, though very complex documents may need minor adjustments.

Q: Can I use it for large files?

A: The tool supports files up to 5 MB. Larger documents may need to be split or compressed first.

Q: Is my file uploaded online?

A: No. The conversion is handled locally in the browser.

Q: Why convert to PDF?

A: PDF is a universal format that preserves layout and is easier to share across devices and systems.
