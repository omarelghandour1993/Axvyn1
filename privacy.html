Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Welcome to Axvyn. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our website and online tools.

1. Information We Collect

Most Axvyn tools are designed to work directly in your browser. Files selected for browser-based tools are processed locally on your device and are not intentionally uploaded to our servers.

We may receive information that your browser automatically sends, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and approximate usage information. This information may be collected by hosting, security, analytics, or advertising providers.

2. How Your Files Are Handled

Tools such as image conversion, image compression, PDF conversion, DOCX conversion, and browser text-to-speech are designed to process files locally in your browser.

You should not upload confidential, sensitive, private, or legally protected files unless you understand how the selected tool processes them.
